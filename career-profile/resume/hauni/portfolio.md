# Overview

At BEO Software, I worked as Team Lead on software platforms for HAUNI, contributing to digital manufacturing and industrial automation solutions used by customers worldwide. This role combined technical leadership, software architecture and hands-on engineering within an international product development environment.

It marked an important step in my career by expanding my experience from healthcare and enterprise software into large-scale industrial engineering systems.
