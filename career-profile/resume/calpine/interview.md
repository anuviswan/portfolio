# Interview Narrative

Calpine Group represents the foundation of my professional career. I joined as a Junior Software Developer and progressed through multiple engineering and leadership roles to become Principal Technical Lead over nearly a decade.

This journey demonstrates continuous technical growth, increasing ownership, engineering leadership and long-term product stewardship.

## Early Career

## Mid Career

Took ownership of increasingly complex functionality, worked directly with international teams, and became involved in technical decision-making and mentoring.

## Leadership

Led offshore engineering activities, managed QA and Release teams, participated in architecture discussions and coordinated with product owners and architects.
