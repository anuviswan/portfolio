# Company Overview

## About the Company

Calpine Group specialized in engineering software solutions for international customers, with a strong focus on scientific, pharmaceutical and laboratory automation systems.

My tenure at Calpine laid the foundation for my career in scientific software engineering. During nearly a decade with the organization, I progressed from a Junior Software Developer to Principal Technical Lead while contributing to globally deployed laboratory automation products and building expertise in software architecture, hardware integration and engineering leadership.
