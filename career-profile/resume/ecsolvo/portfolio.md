EcSolvo Technologies represents the entrepreneurial chapter of my career. As Co-Founder and Chief Software Architect, I combined software architecture, customer engagement and product engineering while helping build a technology business from the ground up.

This experience broadened my perspective beyond software delivery by exposing me to business strategy, customer discovery and solution consulting.
