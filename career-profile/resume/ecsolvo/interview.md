# Interview Narrative

EcSolvo Technologies represents the entrepreneurial chapter of my career. As Co-Founder and Chief Software Architect, I combined software architecture, customer engagement and business leadership while remaining hands-on with engineering.

Unlike my previous roles, I was responsible not only for building software, but also for understanding customer problems, shaping solutions, estimating work, selecting technologies and ensuring successful delivery.

---

# Career Story

## Why EcSolvo?

After years in engineering leadership, I wanted to experience software development from a business owner's perspective. Co-founding EcSolvo gave me the opportunity to understand how technical decisions directly influence customer success, delivery, cost and long-term business sustainability.
