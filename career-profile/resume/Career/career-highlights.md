# Career Overview

Over more than two decades, I have progressed from Junior Software Developer to Principal Engineer, building software across scientific instrumentation, life sciences, healthcare, industrial manufacturing and enterprise platforms.

Throughout this journey I have remained a hands-on engineer while steadily expanding my responsibilities in software architecture, technical leadership, mentoring and product evolution.
