# Overview

As Principal Engineer at BEO Software, I contribute to the evolution of software platforms for KRÜSS, a global provider of scientific instruments used in surface and interface science. My role combines software architecture, technical leadership and hands-on engineering to help build reliable software for laboratories, research organizations and industrial customers.

This position brings together the experience gained throughout my career in scientific software, industrial manufacturing, healthcare systems and enterprise application development.

---

# Highlights

- Serve as Principal Engineer for scientific software platforms.
- Influence architectural direction and long-term platform evolution.
- Collaborate with scientists, product owners, architects and international engineering teams.

# Engineering Philosophy

My approach to engineering is centred on building software that remains valuable for many years. I favour incremental modernization over disruptive rewrites, encourage collaborative architectural decision-making and believe that technical leadership is demonstrated through mentorship, code quality and sustainable engineering practices.

# Lessons Learned

Working on scientific software has reinforced several principles:

- Precision and reliability are fundamental requirements.
- Software architecture should enable long-term product evolution.
- The best technical decisions are made through collaboration with domain experts.
- Engineering excellence is achieved through continuous improvement rather than one-time initiatives.
