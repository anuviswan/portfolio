# Common Interview Questions

## What differentiates a Principal Engineer from a Team Lead?

A Principal Engineer influences technical direction beyond a single team, promotes engineering excellence, mentors engineers and helps shape long-term platform strategy while remaining technically hands-on.

## What have you learned in scientific software?

Scientific software demands precision, reliability and maintainability. Architectural decisions must consider product longevity, usability and close collaboration with domain experts.

## What leadership philosophy do you follow?

Lead through technical credibility, collaboration and mentoring. Help teams make sound engineering decisions rather than simply directing implementation.
