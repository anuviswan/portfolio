# Resume Summary

Principal Engineer, Lead Software Architect and Solution Architect with over 20 years of experience designing and delivering software solutions across scientific instrumentation, life sciences, surface science, industrial manufacturing, healthcare and enterprise domains.

Experienced in leading cross-functional engineering teams, driving software architecture, modernizing mature platforms and collaborating closely with international customers and architects to deliver business-critical solutions. Proven track record spanning desktop, web and cloud technologies, with expertise in scientific instrument integration, laboratory automation, healthcare platforms, industrial analytics and distributed systems.

Passionate about building maintainable software, mentoring engineers and applying pragmatic architectural practices to solve complex real-world engineering problems.
