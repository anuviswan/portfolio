# Overview

At Cascade Revenue Management, I transitioned into a broader engineering leadership role while remaining actively involved in software architecture and development. I led teams delivering healthcare software solutions that supported clinical operations and revenue management workflows.

This role strengthened my experience in balancing technical leadership, people management, software quality and customer collaboration.
