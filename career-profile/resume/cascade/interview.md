# Interview Narrative

Cascade Revenue Management represents an important transition in my career from technical leadership into engineering management while remaining hands-on with software architecture and development.

As Technical Project Manager, I led engineering delivery for healthcare software platforms, balancing technical excellence, people leadership and customer expectations.

# Common Interview Questions

## What changed in this role?

This was my first role with significant responsibility for people leadership while remaining deeply involved in technical architecture and software development.

## What leadership lesson did you learn?

Strong engineering teams are built through mentoring, trust, technical guidance and clear communication—not just project management.

## Why is this role significant?

It prepared me for later Principal Engineer responsibilities by combining architecture, delivery, mentoring and stakeholder management.
